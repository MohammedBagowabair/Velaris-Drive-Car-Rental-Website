Rental requirements

What you need to bring

  • Minimum age 25 (30 for Porsche 911, Taycan and Range Rover)
  • Full driving licence held for at least 3 years
  • Malaysian MyKad, or passport for international guests
  • International Driving Permit if your licence is not in English or Malay
  • Refundable security deposit (amount depends on the car, confirmed on WhatsApp)

FAQ

Good questions

Can you deliver the car to my hotel?

Yes. We deliver across Kuala Lumpur and the Klang Valley, including hotel lobbies, residences and offices. Choose "hotel delivery" or "other address" and share the details on WhatsApp.

Do you meet guests at KLIA or KLIA2?

Yes. Send your flight number and we track it, so the car is waiting at arrivals even if you land late.

Do I need a credit card?

The security deposit can be arranged by card or bank transfer. Our concierge will confirm the details for your car on WhatsApp.

Can I book a chauffeur instead?

Yes. Most of our sedans, SUVs and the Alphard can be booked with a professional chauffeur. Mention it in your WhatsApp message.

Can I drive to Singapore or Thailand?

Cross-border travel is possible for selected cars with advance notice. Ask us before booking so we can prepare the documents.

What happens if something goes wrong?

You get 24/7 WhatsApp support and roadside assistance. If a car ever has an issue, we arrange a replacement as quickly as possible.
